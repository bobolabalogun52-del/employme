# Data Model Notes — Job Seeker / Employer App

- **Users** — email, password, account type (seeker/employer)
- **Job Seekers** — profile info, work experience, education, skills, resume
- **Employers** — company name, description, location, industry
- **Jobs** — title, description, requirements, salary, location, posted by (employer)
- **Applications** — links a seeker to a job, with status (applied/reviewed/hired/rejected)
- **Messages** (optional) — sender, receiver, text, timestamp
